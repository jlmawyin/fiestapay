# Judge walkthrough — draft

October28 candidate instructions. The new presentation and organizer assistant are still under independent review; the public deployment remains9a40f26. Recheck this walkthrough against the final approved release before handing it to judges. Sandbox buyer accounts exist; their private allocation is still a submission preparation item.

Open https://fiestapay.somospanas.com on a phone or desktop browser. The interface is Spanish; these instructions explain the key labels. This is a sandbox demonstration: **no real money and no personal Google account**.

1. Choose the demo entry to create an isolated meal invitation, or use the common Meal, House party, Trip or Event form. Review the dates, organizer display name, contribution and invitation summary before publishing. Save the organizer link shown after creation: it is the secret access to your dashboard. The public invitation is a different link.
2. Open the **public invitation** in another browser or private window. Enter a fictional alias. Choose the affirmative attendance option and one companion: two people altogether. Share only this public link with guests; the WhatsApp button opens a draft and does not send it automatically.
3. Confirm attendance. **Confirmado, sin pagar** means attendance is confirmed but payment is still pending.
4. Ask the assistant about the event or your contribution. Voice requires microphone consent and sends audio to AssemblyAI. Text/form controls remain available if voice fails.
5. Review the server quote: **2 × USD 25 = USD 50**. Confirm the displayed quote before creating a PayPal order.
6. Approve checkout using a designated **sandbox buyer**, never your real PayPal account/card. Buyer credentials and their allocation are pending publication before submission; do not claim this step can be completed without them.
7. After verified capture, return through the saved organizer link. Check one registered guest, two paid people and USD50 collected before fees. The USD50 minimum enables the organizer to confirm that the gathering will take place. Fee estimates belong in the organizer dashboard; the guest reviews only their contribution and amount due. PayPal determines its actual fee.
8. The WhatsApp reminder opens a draft. Nothing is sent automatically. The candidate organizer assistant can explain event information, paid/unpaid attendees and dashboard controls; it must not confirm, cancel or refund on the organizer's behalf.
9. Reset the demo to start a new isolated generation. Historical payment records are retained and cannot credit the new event.

If a provider is unavailable, the UI reports the failure. Canceling checkout or receiving an ambiguous network error is never proof of a successful payment; consult the order state before retrying.

Optional recovery email must be verified before it can recover the organizer link. No email is needed to create or join. If you request recovery, the previous organizer link becomes invalid after the new key is issued. Use only a mailbox you control; no judge mailbox is required for the main walkthrough.

Verified evidence retained from October25–26 includes the USD50 sandbox capture, one automatic USD1 refund after a funding minimum failed, and organizer email recovery. See [payment](reviews/sandbox-evidence.md), [refund](reviews/refund-sandbox-20261026.md) and [email recovery](reviews/smtp-live-20261025.md). These are historical accepted operations, not a claim that you have completed them in a new session. Do not change a live demo's deadline or refund existing evidence to reproduce the demonstration.

The sandbox recipient is the single Business merchant configured for the demo. Capture credits that account; confirming the gathering is an application status, not an escrow release. Connecting each organizer's personal merchant or making an additional transfer through PayPal Payouts is outside this demo. PayPal handles checkout credentials; Buyer Protection depends on the transaction and its applicable terms, rather than covering every payment automatically.

The complete human walkthrough, physical mobile audio and final video remain separate acceptance items. A real voice session requires available provider credits and microphone consent; simulated audio tests do not prove physical playback. Incoming responses should wait in a queue without cutting the audio already playing. Pressing **Detener** explicitly stops playback and the microphone.
