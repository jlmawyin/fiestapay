## Recording checklist

- Use English narration or synchronized English translation. The app can remain in Spanish; explain its labels.
- Record functioning behavior on the actual target device. Label desktop mobile emulation as emulation; it does not establish a physical phone test.
- Set future start/end/deadline values. Keep the same event and payment through quote, approval, verified receipt and dashboard. Record the source version, order/capture reference and verification time privately.
- Do not display buyer passwords, private management links/QR, recovery tokens, browser cookies or provider keys. Capture the app viewport and mask sensitive fields before recording them.
- If checkout exceeds its allotted 27 seconds, retain the real steps and label time cuts; do not edit an unverified result into a success. The 90-second target is not a response-time promise.
- Reserve the ending for the product name and public demo URL. Show receipt/dashboard evidence, not an unimplemented payout.
