## Behavior

- **Explora la demo** offers real read-only AG Grid, a private organizer demo and **Probar pago con PayPal**. **Prueba en vivo** creates an ordinary gathering with a clear sandbox notice.
- The payment entry explicitly creates an isolated owned gathering, saves its organizer key, joins a fictional alias and prepares RSVP for two people. Partial retries retain the known slug; uncertain creation warns rather than automatically creating another. The ordinary server quote and human confirmation precede every order and capture.
- **Ver cuenta de prueba** explicitly fetches only the dedicated test buyer from `/api/demo/buyer`. The opt-in, exact HTTPS sandbox host and no-store response are required. The credential values are intentionally public in this feature, but are not compiled into the bundle, stored in browser storage or logged. Other credentials have no fallback path to this response.
- One orb offers **Escribir** and **Hablar**. Public, guest and organizer scopes stay separate. No voice token, microphone or model call occurs just by opening the selector. Closing or switching to text unmounts the voice pane and invokes the existing cleanup/release logic.
